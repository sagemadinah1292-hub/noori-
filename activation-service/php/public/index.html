YOUR NEXT THREE STEPS

Let’s get you singing.

Download the installer above. If you received a protected build, keep your personal invitation key nearby.

  1. 01

    Install NOORI CHOIR

    Close Cubase and run the downloaded Windows installer. Reopen Cubase, then add NOORI CHOIR to your vocal track.

  2. 02

    Open the plugin

    Find NOORI CHOIR among your VST3 effects and add it to a vocal track. Import a WAV to start preparing your choir parts.

  3. 03

    Register when required

    Protected builds use Settings → Registration / Trial: enter your email and key, then choose Activate. The development preview does not require a key.

A FEW HELPFUL NOTES

Before you
press play.

Need a key or a little help?
Reply to the person who sent your NOORI invitation.

Where do I get my serial number?

NOORI sends invitation keys privately, for example by email. This page does not issue keys automatically. If you did not receive yours, ask the person who shared the plugin with you.

Do I enter my key on this website?

No. Enter it in the plugin’s Registration / Trial screen. This connects activation to your Windows installation. You never need to share your Gmail password.

Can I move my key to another computer?

For protected builds, your key is linked to its first registered email and computer. Contact the NOORI owner to request a computer reset. Reinstalling the plugin does not restart a trial.

Does activation upload my vocals?

No. Registration sends your email, invitation key and a hashed computer identifier to the activation service. Vocal audio is not sent to that service.

What happens when my trial ends?

For protected builds, ask the NOORI owner for free full access. The owner can upgrade the same key. Reconnect to the internet and choose Check online in the plugin to refresh your access.
