YOUR NEXT THREE STEPS

Let’s get you singing.

Keep the installer and invitation key you received from NOORI nearby.

  1. 01

    Install NOORI CHOIR

    Close Cubase and run the Windows installer sent to you. Reopen Cubase, then add NOORI CHOIR to your vocal track.

  2. 02

    Open registration

    In the plugin, open Settings → Registration / Trial. Registration happens inside NOORI CHOIR on your computer.

  3. 03

    Use your invitation

    Enter your email and personal serial number, then choose Activate. Keep your internet connection on during activation and use.

A FEW HELPFUL NOTES

Before you
press play.

Need a key or a little help?
Reply to the person who sent your NOORI invitation.

Where do I get my serial number?

NOORI sends invitation keys privately, for example by email. This page does not issue keys automatically. If you did not receive yours, ask the person who shared the plugin with you.

Do I enter my key on this website?

No. Enter it in the plugin’s Registration / Trial screen. This connects activation to your Windows installation. You never need to share your Gmail password.

Can I move my key to another computer?

Your key is linked to its first registered email and computer. Contact the NOORI owner to request a computer reset. Reinstalling the plugin does not restart a trial.

Does activation upload my vocals?

No. Registration sends your email, invitation key and a hashed computer identifier to the activation service. Vocal audio is not sent to that service.

What happens when my trial ends?

Ask the NOORI owner for free full access. The owner can upgrade the same key. Reconnect to the internet and choose Check online in the plugin to refresh your access.
